Sound samples for Hold the Rift

Everything on this page is made by code in the browser. There are no sound files, so there is nothing to license and nothing to download. Use headphones or speakers. Tell Claude what to keep, change or cut.

1. Battle music

One track with two moods. Calm plays while you build and between waves. Attack adds drums, bass and a lead line while creatures are on the trail. In the game it would switch on its own when you press Start wave.

Intensity
Music and effects drop low while a word is read, so the vowel is easy to hear.

5. A busy battle

Ten seconds of attack music with towers firing at random, the way a late wave would sound. The game caps how many sounds play at once, so it stays punchy and never turns into noise.

Want a real recorded music track instead? Claude can swap in a free (CC0) track. It sounds richer but makes the game larger and needs one download from you.
